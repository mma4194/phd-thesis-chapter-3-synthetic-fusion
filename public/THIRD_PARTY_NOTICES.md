## UMass Smart*

Dataset: Smart*: An Open Data Set and Tools for Enabling Research in Sustainable Homes. Sean Barker, Aditya Mishra, David Irwin, Emmanuel Cecchet, Prashant Shenoy and Jeannie Albrecht. SustKDD 2012.

Source and attribution: https://traces.cs.umass.edu/docs/traces/smartstar/

The source page states CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . This package supplies derived evaluation measurements and code; obtain the original Home A 2013 data from the provider. Transformations include one-minute aggregation, feature/role construction and synthetic evaluation. No provider endorsement is implied.
